## Accuracy boundaries

Little Miami's homepage and FAQ disagree on closing hours; no definitive hours
note was imported. Its FAQ's general no-steps statement is not extended to the
rooftop or separate Event Center. The published service-animal/pet restriction is
attributed and unresolved, not endorsed or treated as a legal rule. The gluten-free
Bass Island condition requires selecting cauliflower crust; cross-contact controls
are unknown. Outdoor heating is not a smoke-free or weather guarantee.

Brink's official pages support food and advance-contact planning but not physical
access. A dog-bowl station was not turned into a human drinking-water feature. Its
public form's demo/configuration text was not treated as a functioning contact
promise. The email address is a public venue contact, not a response guarantee.

Streetside's official pages do not claim a complete step-free route. A separate
public comment on r/FCCincinnati mentions no steps and parking across from the
entrance. Exact publication and visit dates remain null. Door widths, crossings,
parking bays and restroom fittings are not independently verified. Source:
https://www.reddit.com/r/FCCincinnati/comments/1jvwc9z/comment/mmes2x9/

ARCO's four-floor elevator statement does not erase its explicit inaccessible
second-floor balcony. Wheelchair visitors are directed to the west or adjacent
first-floor north entrances. There is no first-floor restroom. Baby changing
stations do not establish adult changing equipment; room/stage access and lift
outage arrangements remain gaps. Maps and empty-room photographs do not promise
an event's seating layout.
