# Additional venue photograph evidence

Checked 10 September 2026. Six additional catalog venues now have photographs from individually inspected Commons file pages with explicit reuse terms. These are actual venue photographs, not accessibility observations. Source-reported capture dates are separate from the retrieval date, and older images are clearly captioned as historical context. Existing photo manifests and files were preserved.

Inputs and source HTML are stored under the ignored `output/image-sources/extra/` directory. Supplied Commons display versions were used to avoid unnecessary large downloads. Each actual input was inspected at full size before conversion. Public images retain the full downloaded frame; no scene content was altered.

Display author, source and license links, and the existing resize/WebP conversion notice. Each CC BY-SA image and its conversion remain under the stated image license; the site code license does not replace image licenses. CC0 author credit is retained for provenance. Credit does not imply endorsement or a verified organization account.

## Selection notes

The initial “Cincinnati Zoo Sign.jpg” candidate showed an animal-contact warning and was rejected after visual inspection in favor of identifiable zoo signage. A historical 1875 zoo postcard was also excluded. Search matches for Sharon Woods in the Columbus area were excluded; none were substituted for the Hamilton County park. The alternate Contemporary Arts Center 2019 file explicitly warns against cropping to emphasize Metrobot; the selected broad streetscape also keeps that sculpture incidental and must remain full-frame.

An official Winton Lake closure notice surfaced during photo research. The coordinating agent subsequently verified the superseding [24 June reopening announcement](https://www.greatparks.org/about/newsroom/great-parks-reopens-winton-woods-boathouse-after-restoring-water-based-recreation-at-winton-lake), so the earlier closure was not added to the listing. Neither water availability nor current bridge condition is inferred from the 2004 photograph.
