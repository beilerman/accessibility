# Ohio venue photograph evidence

Checked 10 September 2026. Five exact-venue photographs were selected from individually inspected Wikimedia Commons file pages with explicit reuse terms. This is imagery provenance, not an accessibility audit. These historical images are venue context and cannot establish current routes, measurements, facilities, consensus, or organization verification.

The downloaded JPEG files were each opened with `view_image` at original detail before conversion. Three downloads are source originals; two use Commons-supplied 1,280-pixel versions following original-download rate limits. Temporary inputs are outside the repository at `/tmp/threshold-oh-photo-originals/`. Each delivered image retains the entire downloaded frame.

Display author credit, a source link, a license link, and a notice that images were resized and converted to WebP alongside the photographs. The CC BY-SA derivatives remain under each image’s listed license; the application license does not replace these image licenses. The public-domain image retains author credit for provenance. No endorsement is implied.

The image dates are source-reported capture dates, separate from the research check date. Approximate or conflicting dates are preserved below.

## Excluded searches

Search results for “Aronoff Center” predominantly depicted the University of Cincinnati DAAP building rather than the downtown Aronoff Center for the Arts. Pyramid Hill matches depicted sites in Australia and elsewhere rather than the Ohio sculpture park. Those mismatches were excluded. No venue website photograph was treated as reusable merely because it was publicly visible.
