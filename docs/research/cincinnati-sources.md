# Greater Cincinnati Ohio public-source research

Checked: 2026-09-10. Dataset: `cincinnati.json` (18 venues, 55 facts).

These are summaries of official public webpages, not in-person measurements, accessibility audits, verified organization submissions, certification by Threshold, or guarantees. No photographs were acquired. Domain tags organize published details; they must not be converted into blanket suitability ratings. Unmentioned features remain unknown, not absent. Cities follow the venue's published postal location where an address was inspected.

Use every fact's source_url and the record's checked_at in the interface. Keep limitations visible. Avoid labeling these records “audited,” “verified accessible,” “community reviewed,” or “claimed by owner.” The checked date refers to source review, not an inspection date.

## Research issues and next checks

- Bettman Nature Preserve: official park search result provided the path claim, but repeat full-page retrieval timed out. Keep its limitation or exclude from the initial release if full-page capture is required.
- Pyramid Hill: the same source both describes a wheelchair-accessible Gallery Museum entrance and mentions a small step. This conflict is intentionally visible; do not infer a confirmed step-free route.
- Sharon Woods: the system accessibility page explicitly excludes Sharon Woods from its general accessible paved-trail statement. Its park page also reports harbor work. Do not generalize the accessible playground designation to the entire park.
- Winton Woods: the accessibility page lists an accessible launch, while the current paddling page's dedicated accessible-launch section names only Miami Whitewater. No closure was established, so confirm Winton operation before using a live-availability claim.
- Playhouse: the current primary `/visit/accessibility` page was inspected; the indexed template-host result was not used as the final source. Parking reservations and performance-specific services matter.
- Freedom Center: the sensory-cart article displays a 2025 date while the voices index shows a 2026 listing. No publication date was inferred; only the 2026-09-10 checked date is stored.
- Sports venues were investigated but omitted to preserve a concise, primary-source dataset without overextending restricted source summaries.
- Kings Island's updated accessibility hub was inspected, but its expandable content was not fully exposed. Omitted rather than extrapolating older admission-program rules.

## Geographic and content coverage

The dataset covers central Cincinnati plus Hamilton, Harrison, Sharonville and Milford, including libraries, arts institutions, a zoo and parks. It is a selected starter collection, not comprehensive coverage. Northern Kentucky is being researched separately. Missing restaurant, workplace, medical-office and everyday retail data are future coverage opportunities; public evidence must establish individual features before inclusion.
