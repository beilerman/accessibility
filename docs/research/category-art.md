# Category artwork

Generated and visually inspected: 2026-09-10.

## Purpose and provenance

Five active category images, plus one preserved superseded train variant, generated with OpenAI's built-in `image_gen` tool. No reference images or actual venue photos were supplied. The built-in tool did not expose the exact backend model/version. Each image is visibly stylized cut-paper/gouache editorial art in warm cream, deep teal, forest green, sage and muted terracotta. All depict fictional generic subjects, with no words, logos, people, recognizable real landmarks or access/compliance symbols.

Use these only as category artwork on cards where an appropriate real venue photograph is unavailable. Every display must show the readable caption from `src/data/category-art.json`, beginning **AI artwork**, and state that it is not a view of the venue. Do not label these as venue photographs or visual evidence of a location's access conditions. Keep existing photo provenance distinct.

## Visual review

The first generations of the four non-transport categories were selected after visual inspection. The transportation category was subsequently replaced with airport-themed artwork to fit the current catalog. The set is cohesive, clearly illustrated, sharp at card resolution and free from baked text. Library books/table, park trees/path, gallery arch/art, theater curtains/chairs and airport terminal/airliner are readily distinguishable. The fictional stage, paths, furniture, train and platform do not establish accessible routes, transfer surfaces, safe dimensions, operating conditions, amenities, ramps, lifts or compliance at any venue. The active transportation art shows a parked generic airliner beyond terminal windows; it supplies no boarding-access information. The earlier train image remains saved but is no longer referenced by category-art.json. No architectural or product validation is claimed.

Originals: 1536 × 1024 PNG, retained unchanged in ignored `output/imagegen/category-*-original.png`. Web assets: uncropped 1200 × 800 WebP at quality 85, converted using Sharp. No visual content was edited after generation. Metadata dimensions were checked after conversion and all web assets were visually inspected.
