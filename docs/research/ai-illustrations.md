## Visual quality review

- **Entrance:** selected first generation. Straight, coherent frame; doors appear slid aside; open central passage and hard floor without a visible step. A thin metal transition is visible and may not be perfectly flush; no actual sill-height, slope, width, slip-resistance, glass-marking or sensor/door-operation claim is made. Furniture far inside does not block the depicted foreground entrance, but the image cannot establish the complete route.
- **Sensory kit:** selected first generation. Exactly two padded cups joined by a continuous headband and plausible adjustment brackets; separate six-bubble fidget and zip pouch. No broken connections, duplicate objects, medicines, readable labels or people. Fit, attenuation, material safety and cleaning cannot be determined from a generated image.
- **Rest area:** selected first generation. Chair has a back, two armrests and four visible legs; clear floor beside it and an unobstructed foreground. No claim about load capacity, seating ergonomics, wheelchair turning space, measured illumination, sound level or a venue's availability.

All three originals are 1536 × 1024 PNG. Web copies are uncropped 1200 × 800 WebP at quality 88. Original bytes were copied without alteration; only web format/size conversion used Sharp. No generated objects were retouched. The 3:2 composition is retained so visible approach space is not removed.
