## Exclusions and interpretation

- Behringer-Crawford Museum: search indexing exposed a parking claim, but its FAQ returned 404 when opened. Excluded rather than relying on a broken page.
- Campbell County Library: homepage fetch returned 403 and search did not provide a robust current accessibility page; excluded.
- Old Kenton County parks material dated 2009 was excluded. A 2026 county notice describes an impending Fox Ridge closure; planned features were not treated as operating amenities.
- BCPL's June 9, 2026 post explicitly names Main, Scheben, Hebron, Florence and Walton for wheelchair availability. It only says most branches have automatic doors, so no branch-specific automatic-door assertion was generated. A coming-soon Memory Cafe was not treated as operational. Official branch addresses also checked at https://www.bcpl.org/locations .
- CVG locality confirmed at https://www.cvgairport.com/directions-and-traffic/ . Main official mailing address is in Ohio; the physical airport is in Hebron, Kentucky. Address omitted from data to avoid mailing/physical ambiguity.
- Big Bone Lick: state park page is the primary source for trails and pool closure; NPS adds amenity reporting but does not locate or measure the accessible restroom. No overall wheelchair-accessible label. NPS and reservation-system street numbering differed, so address omitted.
- Florence Aquatic Center has a seasonal closure caveat. Accessible zero-depth pool does not imply access to other attractions.
- The Carnegie and BB Riverboats retain explicit upper-level barriers. These venues must not receive an unqualified all-access label.
