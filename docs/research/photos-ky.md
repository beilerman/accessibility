# Northern Kentucky venue photo evidence

Checked: 2026-09-10. Four accepted real photographs for four existing catalog places. Each has an explicit file-level Commons license or CC0 dedication. Public availability alone was not treated as reuse permission.

These provide dated venue context. They do not verify a current entrance, accessible route, doorway width, grade, boarding procedure, facility availability, or audit result. Capture dates come from photographer/source metadata.

## Processing and display

Original JPEGs and Commons API metadata are preserved locally in gitignored `pipeline/staging/photos-ky-originals/`. Sharp applied EXIF orientation, proportional resize to a maximum width of 1600 pixels without enlargement, and WebP quality 84. No cropping, retouching, color correction, structural alteration, or generated fill. People, signs, barriers and objects remain in frame.

Display the author, linked source, linked license, capture date, and this changes notice: "Resized and converted to WebP; full frame retained."

Creation Museum's WebP retains CC BY-SA 4.0; retain that license with distributed adaptations. Newport Aquarium carries CC BY 2.0 attribution. Carnegie and Belle of Cincinnati are CC0; credits are retained for traceability. These image licenses do not license the site or imply photographer/venue endorsement.
