# Community visitor reports, November 2026

Checked November 8, 2026. Three research passes covered sports and concert venues; attractions and museums; and parks, transit and public spaces. Sources were Reddit, Tripadvisor, personal and disability-travel blogs.

## Method

- Only firsthand accounts, or a companion's account of a shared visit, with concrete access detail. Generic praise, marketing, news, AI-written summaries and wrong-venue matches were rejected.
- Every accepted account was opened and its anchor passage matched on the live page by Threshold, not only by the research agent. Reddit blocks scripts, so Reddit comments were read on `embed.reddit.com`, with authors and dates confirmed through the Arctic Shift archive. Tripadvisor was read in a regular browser.
- Summaries are under 80 words, neutral and in Threshold's words. Names of companions and children are left out. Each caveat states the date, perspective and anything the account leaves unclear. Where a report conflicts with official information or a Threshold review, the caveat says so.

## Accepted (25 reports, 16 places)

Kings Island (3), Ark Encounter (2), Vent Haven Museum, the Connector streetcar, Metro buses, CVG, Sharon Woods, Winton Woods, Cincinnati Nature Center, Devou Park (2), Downtown Main Library (2, one noisy and one quiet), Great American Ball Park (2), Riverbend Music Center (2), Paycor Stadium, Heritage Bank Center (3) and Bogart's.

## Rejected

- Search snippets that could not be opened (a Museum Center Tripadvisor review), and a Tripadvisor mirror link readers could not use.
- Thin or contradicted one-liners: a Taft Theatre review saying the venue is "not handicap accessible" against the theater's documented accessible entrance and seating; a Smale Riverfront Park Q&A answer.
- Older accounts where newer ones existed: 2016 Aronoff and 2018 Paycor reviews; a 2021 Kings Island pass account superseded by 2023–2024 ones.
- A deleted May 2026 Kings Island post; a Kings Island ambassador's sponsored blog; posts with children's medical details.

## Still uncovered

No usable firsthand accounts were found for Metro Access paratransit, TANK or RAMP, TQL Stadium (beyond Threshold's own review), Truist Arena, MegaCorp Pavilion, Music Hall, Fountain Square, Washington Park, Smale, Ault Park, the Freedom Center, Krohn Conservatory and several smaller venues. Reddit full-text search was rate-limited, so some subreddits (r/disability, r/wheelchairs, r/autism) were not fully searched.
